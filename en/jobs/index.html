Working at Milecrew

Are you tired of boring routine work and unproductive meetings? Do you want to contribute your individual strengths to specific milestones and be part of a bigger picture? Are you ready to develop yourself further, learn something new every day and enjoy variety instead of routine?

Then you’ve come to the right place. Welcome to Milecrew.

The crew finds you.

Strong Members – Strong Crew

When you come on board with us, we give you a guarantee: you will learn a huge amount.

Because as a crew, we always set course together, never alone. We complement each other in our strengths, specializations and areas of focus. We learn from each other and support each other in achieving new goals and expanding our skills. This is made possible by our combined professional experience, which you can feel in every corner of Milecrew, and by the variety of our projects and the way in which we implement them.

We don’t do the classic recruiting blah-blah – you talk directly to the people you’ll later work with as a crew. We usually find our crew members ourselves: if you’re good enough for Milecrew, we’ll find you – or one of our AI agents will. Still, we’re always happy to get to know great talent.

We play fair anyway: a market-rate salary package, a permanent contract and the option of a company pension scheme are a matter of course. Plus a crew that simply makes work fun.
